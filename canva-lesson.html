What is Canva?

Goal: explain what Canva is and what you can make with it.

Canva is a free, drag-and-drop design tool that works in your web browser. You do not need to draw. You pick a template, change the words and pictures, and make it yours.

Before you start

Go to canva.com, sign up with your school email (ask your teacher or parent first), then choose Create a design. Every design starts with a blank page or a template.

Shortcuts and Safe Sharing

Goal: use 4 quick shortcuts and share your work responsibly.

Select an element on your canvas, then try these (on Mac, use Cmd instead of Ctrl).

  • Ctrl + C and Ctrl + V: copy and paste
  • Ctrl + Z: undo a mistake
  • Delete: remove the element
  • Ctrl + D: duplicate
